# Country Holidays Hotels & Resorts — Comprehensive Knowledge Guide

## General Overview
Country Holidays Hotels & Resorts is an ultra-luxury hospitality collective with sanctuaries located across pristine coastal cliffs, high-altitude alpine pine forests, historic royal palaces, and ancient spice plantations.

## Signature Experiences & Curated Itineraries
- **Private Astronomy Sessions**: Equipped with high-powered Meade telescopes and guided by our resident astrophysicist on the cedarwood viewing decks.
- **Botanical Plantation Foraging**: Guided herbal walking tours with Ayurvedic practitioners to discover rare indigenous flora, cardamom, and cinnamon trees.
- **Heli-Yoga & Cliffside Meditation**: Sunrise meditation sessions conducted on cantilevered slate platforms hovering over mist valleys.
- **Subterranean Wine Cellar Tastings**: Sommelier-guided 5-course wine pairing sessions in natural subterranean rock vaults.

## Airport & Heli Transfers
- **Helicopter Direct Transfers**: Available from nearest international airports with advance reservation (48 hours prior notice required).
- **Private Chauffeur Fleet**: Luxury Mercedes-Maybach and Range Rover airport transfers with high-speed in-car Wi-Fi, refreshments, and bespoke luggage handling.

## Guest Dress Code & Resort Etiquette
- **Daywear**: Elegant casual / resort chic across all pavilions and garden trails.
- **Evening Dining**: Smart casual required for The Cliffside Pavilion & Hearth and Sommelier Wine Vault (formal footwear requested, swimwear strictly prohibited in dining venues).
